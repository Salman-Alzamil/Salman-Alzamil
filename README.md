# Salman Alzamil

AI engineer in Riyadh. I build retrieval and LLM systems, computer vision models, and the web and mobile apps that use them. B.Sc. in Computer Science from Imam Mohammad Ibn Saud Islamic University.

## Projects

### [Mirqat](https://github.com/Salman-Alzamil/mirqat)
Arabic and English question answering over 52 books of Hanbali fiqh and hadith. Every answer cites the book, volume and page it came from. Live at [mirqat.app](https://mirqat.app).

### [Freelance work](https://github.com/Salman-Alzamil/freelance-work)
Two platforms in production for clients. One handles program registration and online payments for a community association. The other is a finance system for an education consultancy that reads invoices with a vision model.

### [Marrow](https://github.com/Salman-Alzamil/marrow)
A mobile app that takes a shared TikTok, Instagram or YouTube link and turns the video into a recipe, workout or travel plan.

### [ECHO](https://salman-alzamil.github.io/ECHO/)
Pronunciation practice. You say a line back and see your pitch drawn against the target, with a score for pitch and rhythm.

### [Skylight](https://salman-alzamil.github.io/skylight/)
A weather app drawn on a live WebGL2 sky, written in plain JavaScript with no build step.

I also run a multi-agent system on my own machine that handles research and scheduled reporting.
